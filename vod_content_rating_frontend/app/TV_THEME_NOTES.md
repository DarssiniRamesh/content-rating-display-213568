TV Theme Notes

- Base TV theme: @style/Theme.VodCalificador.TV
- Convenience alias: @style/AppTheme.TV (values-tv/styles-alias.xml)
- Overlays: @style/Theme.VodCalificador.TV.Overlays can be used for default button/text styles.
- Apply via manifest overlay to TV activities:
  <activity android:name="...TvIndexActivity" android:theme="@style/AppTheme.TV"/>
